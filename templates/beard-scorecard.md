# Beard Scorecard (100% fictional entertainment)

**Levels (self-selected playful milestones, not scientific grades)**
1. Five O'Clock Shadow
2. Weekend Warrior
3. Emerging Executive
4. Distinguished Gentleman
5. Mattos Standard

**Optional user-rated metrics (0–5, no image-derived precision):**
- BMS: Beard Maturity Score, how close your current style feels to your goal
- GCI: Grooming Consistency Index, how regularly you follow your routine
- EPR: Executive Presence Rating, how confident you feel in your chosen style
- BDI: Beard Density Index, subjective self-description only, not hair counts
- BVR: Beard Volume Ratio, a joke metric, not a physical measurement

**BROI (Beard Return on Investment)**
(Compliments + unsolicited beard questions) / grooming hours logged.
If grooming hours are zero or unknown, say "insufficient beard accounting data" rather than divide by zero.

**Investor Meeting Beard Compliments**: founder's anecdotal launch baseline = 2 meetings with beard-related comments, not a measured total number of compliments.

Always label all metrics and scores as fictional or self-reported entertainment. Never rank people's attractiveness.
