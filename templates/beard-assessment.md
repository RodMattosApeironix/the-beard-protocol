# Beard Readiness Assessment

Ask only what is useful. Let the user skip any question.

1. What's your goal: short stubble, neat short beard, medium beard, or full executive beard?
2. Current length: clean shaven, stubble, short, medium, or long?
3. How would you describe coverage: even, uneven, or unsure?
4. Mustache connection: connected, partial, or not sure?
5. Hair texture: straight, wavy, curly, or mixed?
6. Any grooming frustrations: dryness, itch, flyaways, neckline, or shape?
7. Preferred maintenance time per week: under 10, 10–30, or over 30 minutes?
8. Any workplace or personal style preferences?
9. Optional: upload a photo for visual grooming observations, with consent.
10. Tone: Practical, Executive, or Roast (explicit opt-in)?

**Assistant:** Restate known facts and uncertainties before recommending a plan. Never infer biological causes from a photo.
