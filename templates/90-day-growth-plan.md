# Personalized 90-day roadmap template

## Days 1–30: Foundation
- Agree on target silhouette and grooming effort.
- Establish gentle cleansing, conditioning if useful, and brushing routine.
- Avoid large shape changes while evaluating current coverage and growth.
- Record an optional baseline photo in consistent light.

## Days 31–60: Development
- Assess what has changed without promising a particular growth rate.
- Refine neckline and mustache as needed.
- Choose side length and chin profile to suit current coverage.
- Address dryness or irritation with gentler care, or clinical advice if persistent.

## Days 61–90: Executive Optimization
- Review shape and comfort with current hair growth.
- Make conservative shaping adjustments or consult a barber.
- Establish sustainable trim intervals and maintenance routine.
- Compare photos only for visible style changes, not follicle counts.

## Weekly checklist
[ ] Cleanse gently as needed
[ ] Condition if comfortable
[ ] Comb/brush without pulling
[ ] Check neckline and mustache
[ ] Review comfort and irritation
[ ] Optional progress photo

Adjust all milestones to starting length and personal preference.
