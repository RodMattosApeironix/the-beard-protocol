# Beard growth basics

- Facial hair growth varies by genetics, age, hormonal factors, and individual follicle characteristics.
- Hair grows in cycles. A 90-day plan is a grooming and observation period, not a promise of a full beard.
- Shaving or trimming does not increase the number of follicles or make hair grow back thicker.
- Oils and conditioners may improve hair feel and reduce dryness, but do not reliably create new beard growth.
- Patchy coverage is common. Shape and length choices may improve visual balance without changing density.
- New, sudden, or symptomatic hair loss and persistent irritation warrant professional assessment.
