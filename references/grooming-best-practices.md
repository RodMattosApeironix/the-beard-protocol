# Grooming basics

1. Cleanse facial hair and the skin beneath with a gentle product suited to skin type.
2. Pat dry. Use a small amount of conditioner or beard oil if it suits the skin; patch test if sensitive.
3. Comb or brush gently. Avoid pulling, aggressive heat, and harsh products.
4. Trim conservatively and check symmetry in natural light.
5. Keep the neckline intentional and the mustache comfortable around the lip.
6. Document progress under similar lighting if desired. Avoid comparing against altered or filtered photos.
7. Adjust frequency based on growth rate, skin comfort, and preferred appearance.
