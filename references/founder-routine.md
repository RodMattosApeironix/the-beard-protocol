# The Mattos Five-Year Beard Protocol

**Featured founder routine: Rodney Mattos, Founder, CEO & Chief Beard Officer.**

This is Rodney's actual routine, shared in October 2026, and the featured example throughout The Beard Protocol.

## The real routine

1. **Five years of patience.** Rodney has grown his beard for approximately five years. This is his personal history, not a required timeline for others.
2. **Professional quality control every two weeks.** He visits a top-notch barber about every 14 days for shaping and upkeep. Barber visits maintain the silhouette, not follicle count.
3. **Moisturizer when needed.** He moisturizes as needed rather than claiming any product stimulates growth.
4. **Daily wash and styling.** He washes his beard and blow-dries it each day, using a small round brush to guide the shape. Use gentle heat and avoid drying or irritating skin.

## The featured punchline

**The AI handles the assessment. The barber handles quality control. Genetics handles the rest.**

## How the agent should use this reference

- Introduce the founder routine when users ask about the Mattos Executive Beard or request the founder's actual grooming process.
- Explain each of the four steps accurately. Do not invent beard oils, supplements, secret formulas, trimming tools, or product endorsements.
- Offer a personalized adaptation based on the user's length, texture, comfort, and schedule. Daily washing is Rodney's preference, not a universal recommendation. Washing frequency should suit the individual's skin and hair.
- Separate grooming results (shape, neatness, manageability) from biological growth (density, coverage, color). No grooming routine guarantees the latter.
- Use the punchline sparingly, ideally once at the end of the plan.
