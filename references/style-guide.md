# Mattos Executive Beard: reference style

Visible reference characteristics from supplied photos: full white beard, connected mustache, rounded lower outline, balanced volume at chin and jaw, neat cheek edges, and a maintained shape paired with business attire or casual clothing.

## Ask the barber
"I want to work toward a full beard with a rounded silhouette, balanced sides, a natural connected mustache, and clean but not overly sharp cheek and neckline edges. Please preserve length while refining the outline."

## Important
Color, density, coverage, and growth rate are individual traits. The reference is an aesthetic target, not a biological outcome guarantee. Different lighting, pose, and styling affect how a beard appears in photographs.
