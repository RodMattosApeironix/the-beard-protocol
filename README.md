# THE BEARD PROTOCOL 🧔

**Built with AI. Grown with patience. Approved by absolutely no regulatory agency.**

An open-source, good-humored AI agent skill for beard planning, grooming, and an entirely unserious executive maturity scorecard. Inspired by questions asked at actual investor meetings.

## Quick start

1. Download this repository.
2. Copy the `SKILL.md` and `references/` directory into your AI assistant's supported skills folder, or paste `SKILL.md` into a chat and ask the assistant to follow it. Specific installation differs by assistant.
3. Say: **Run my Beard Protocol assessment.**
4. Answer the intake questions. A photo is optional.
5. Receive a grooming plan, 90-day milestones, and optional humorous scorecard.

## What you get

- Personalized, realistic beard styling and maintenance guidance
- Optional photo-based observations, limited to visible features
- A 90-day roadmap and maintenance checklist
- A five-level *fictional* beard maturity model
- Optional, consent-based, good-natured Roast Mode
- Fictional Beard ROI and Investor Compliment KPIs

## Reference look

The **Mattos Executive Beard** is a full white beard with a connected mustache, rounded profile, tidy cheeklines and an intentional silhouette. It's a style reference, not a promised growth outcome. See `assets/` for reference photos.

## Safety and honesty

Beard growth and density depend substantially on genetics, age, and hormones. Trimming, oils and grooming do not create new follicles. No medical diagnoses, prescriptions, or guaranteed results. For sudden hair loss, skin inflammation, or persistent symptoms, seek a qualified clinician. Photos are optional and should not be uploaded without the subject's permission. No facial recognition or identity verification.
