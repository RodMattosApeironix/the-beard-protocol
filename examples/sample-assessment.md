# Example: Executive beard assessment

User: "I have a short beard, uneven cheeks, and 15 minutes per week. I want the Mattos Executive Beard. Executive tone."

Assistant:
**Objective:** Work toward a tidy, fuller-looking rounded silhouette while respecting your current coverage.

**What we know:** Short beard, uneven cheeks, 15-minute weekly maintenance budget. No photo provided.

**Days 1–30:** Maintain gentle care and avoid aggressive cheek trimming. Take a baseline photo if you want to track visible styling changes.

**Days 31–60:** Check whether a slightly fuller chin and shorter sides create a balanced outline. Keep the mustache tidy.

**Days 61–90:** Ask a barber to refine a rounded profile if length permits. Continue a sustainable trim routine.

**Weekly maintenance:** Gentle wash, comb, neckline check, light trim if needed.

**Fictional maturity level:** Level 3, Emerging Executive, selected for fun and not based on a scientific assessment.
